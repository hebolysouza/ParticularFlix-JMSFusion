JMSFusion 4.0.0.17 for Jellyfin 12 (ParticularFlix fork). Phones and header release: no more flashing slider cards on phones, a usable settings panel on phones, phones can show the same slider cards as the desktop, and library tab fixes. Rolls up the unpublished builds 4.0.0.14 to 4.0.0.16. Same plugin GUID, settings are kept.

## 📦 Install

- Catalog URL: `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hebolysouza/ParticularFlix-JMSFusion/main/manifest.json`
- Or manual: put the three files of `JMSFusion-4.0.0.17-server.zip` (DLL, `meta.json`, `icon.png`) in the plugin folder and restart Jellyfin.
- Requires Jellyfin 12.x (targetAbi 12.0.0.0, net10.0). Validated on 12.3. Jellyfin 10.11.x: stay on 3.8.2.45.

## ⬆️ Upgrade notes

- **Reload every open tab after updating** (phones included: close and reopen the app or tab).
- The header changes live in `extras/particularflix-modern-header.js`. On Jellyfin 12 it runs from the JMS `custom` folder (`<config>/data/plugins/configurations/JMSFusion/custom/`), not from the DLL: copy the new file there.
- To make phones show the same slider cards as the desktop, use "Copy the slider cards to phones" on the plugin page (JMSFusion tab). It copies only the API Query settings; the phone layout stays.

## 📱 Phones

- **No more flashing slider cards**: the new card was drawn in pieces for a moment (badges without text, half of the image, the empty card background) because the neighbor card was kept scaled down, with its image shifted and its content hidden, and everything changed at the instant of the swap. The active card and its neighbors now sit on their own layers and the neighbor content is already drawn; the swap only changes opacity and position.
- **Sharper next card**: the full-quality image of the neighbor card is requested right away instead of waiting in the background queue.
- **Settings (F2) on phones**: the panel fills the screen above the app bar, the title and the close button have their own row at the top, and Save/Apply stays pinned at the bottom.
- **Same slider cards as the desktop**: "Copy the slider cards to phones" copies only the settings that decide which cards the slider shows (source, query, count, order, random and manual list) from the desktop default to the phone default. "Undo the last change of the phone default" restores the previous phone default. API: `POST /Plugins/JMSFusion/UserSettings/CopyGlobal` with `keys`; `ResetUser` accepts `userId: "*"` (every non-administrator user).

## 🧭 Header

- **Library icons no longer flash their names** after a click (the icon-only style now comes from CSS).
- **Shared collection libraries**: libraries whose name starts with "Acervo" (configurable with `localStorage pf:navAcervo`) keep the icon with an amber dot and show their full name in a label on hover or focus, above the library toolbar.
- **Library toolbar centered on desktop** (sort, filter, A-Z and pagination); phones keep the Jellyfin layout.

## ⚠️ Known limitations

- The phone flash depends on the device GPU; the fix was validated on the owner's phone. Desktop browsers never showed it.
- The C# changes of this release were built and validated on the owner's test and production servers; there is no automated test suite.
